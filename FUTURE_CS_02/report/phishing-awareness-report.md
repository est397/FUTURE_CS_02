## 4. Examples of phishing indicators
- Sender address and display-name inconsistencies
- Unexpected requests for passwords, codes, or payment
- Urgent or threatening language
- Unexpected links or attachments
- Domain names that imitate trusted organizations

## 5. Prevention guidance
1. Verify unusual requests using a known phone number or official website.
2. Do not share passwords or one-time codes.
3. Avoid opening unexpected attachments.
4. Report suspicious emails to the responsible IT team.
5. Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
